# Benchmark

Run on 2026-11-22 over **60 synthetic GST invoices** generated by `lekha bench` (22 digital pdfs, 22 clean scans, 16 phone photos; 16 carry a signed e-invoice QR). Each has exact ground truth. Reproduce with `uv run lekha bench --n 60 --seed 2026 && uv run lekha bench-eval`.

AI provider: **gemini** — models that served requests: gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemini-3.6-flash.

Share of key fields exactly right (GSTINs, invoice number, date, place of supply, taxable value, IGST/CGST/SGST, total, IRN; amounts within ₹1).

## Speed

- OCR median: 9.0 s per document (laptop CPU, process pool).
- AI extraction median: 4.7 s per document (free tier, includes failover).
- AI unavailable for 14 of 60 documents (every free-tier model rate-limited or timing out at the time of the run). AI rows are scored only on documents the AI answered; in the product, those documents fall back to the offline reader automatically.
- Signed-QR corrections applied: 2.

## Confidence calibration

Field confidence weights were refitted on 506 (signals, correct?) pairs. Brier score 0.0135 → 0.0118 (lower is better). The new weights were saved to `engines/confidence_weights.json`.

## Honest limits

- Synthetic invoices use four layouts and generated degradation; real bills are more varied (handwriting, stamps, multi-page, regional languages). Treat these numbers as a regression baseline, not a guarantee.
- Free-tier AI capacity varies by hour; failover to smaller models can lower accuracy at busy times.
- Every field still passes deterministic GST validation and, by default, human review.
