# User guide

## Set up (once)

1. **Create a workspace** — choose *CA / accounting firm* (many clients) or *My business*.
2. **Add clients** — legal name and GSTIN(s). Lekha validates the GSTIN as you type.
3. **Invite your team** (Settings) — partners, staff, viewers, or a client who sees only their own business.
4. Optional: **import an existing purchase register** (CSV/Excel from Tally, Busy or a spreadsheet) (Purchase register → **Import register**), and set supplier details (MSME status, email/WhatsApp, language) on the Suppliers page.

## Every month

| When | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Any time | Upload bills — drag-and-drop, phone camera, or the client's **upload link** (no login) | Inbox |
| As bills are read | Review: lowest confidence first; click a field to see where it was read; click text on the bill to fill a field; **A** to approve, **J/K** to move | Review |
| After the 14th | Download GSTR-2B (JSON or Excel) from the GST portal and **Import GSTR-2B** | Reconciliation |
| Automatically | Lekha reconciles, recommends IMS actions, checks risk, drafts follow-ups and prepares Table 4 — the **Monthly close** stops for your approval | Monthly close |
| Before the 13th | Apply IMS actions (or approve them in the close) and export them for the portal | IMS actions |
| Before the 20th | Check **Credit at risk**, pay suppliers nearing 180 days (**Payables**), finalise **Table 4**, export to Tally | Credit at risk · Payables · Table 4 · Dashboard |
| Ongoing | Send supplier follow-ups (they reply on their own page); watch **Fraud signals** | Suppliers · Fraud signals |

## When a notice arrives

Notices → **Record a notice** (form, periods, amount). Lekha rebuilds each period, explains the difference with evidence, drafts the reply and prepares a ZIP of proofs. Edit the reply, download, review with your CA, file.

## When the law changes

GST rules → **Draft change** on the affected rule with its effective date and notification number → **Simulate impact** to see which invoices and how many rupees are affected → **Publish**. Every client is re-checked.

## Ask Lekha

The **Ask Lekha** button (bottom right) answers questions about the current client in English, Hindi or Telugu — e.g. "Which suppliers are costing us the most credit?" It only reads data.
